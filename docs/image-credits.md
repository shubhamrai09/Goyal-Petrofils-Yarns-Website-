# Image credits & licensing

All photographs in `src/assets/images/` were downloaded from **Unsplash** using the
`license=free` filter and are covered by the [Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license):
free for commercial use, no permission or attribution required. Unsplash+ (paid) and
Getty-sponsored images were deliberately excluded. Credits are listed anyway as good practice.

**None of these photos show GeeTex's own plants, machinery or products.** They are
representative imagery. Replace them with real factory photography when available
(see `docs/reference-content-inventory.md` §8).

No image, logo or product photo is hotlinked from geetexyarn.com. The only asset reused
from the reference site is the company's **own logo** (`public/brand/geetex-logo.png`, trimmed of
whitespace, not redrawn) and the company's **own chenille shade-card PDF**
(`public/downloads/geetex-chenille-13nm-shade-card.pdf`). The favicon is a crop of the logo's "G" mark.
